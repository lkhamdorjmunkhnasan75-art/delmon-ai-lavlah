# Authorized ingestion MVP
A trusted administrator registers an exact HTTPS source, organization, authorization evidence, expiry and freshness policy. An internal collector can submit a bounded batch of structured public fields only. Normalized content is fingerprinted per permission. A separate reviewer checks evidence and explicitly approves or rejects each candidate. Approved snapshots are published transactionally through the existing directory. Expired observations cannot be approved; permission revocation or expiration removes linked listings from public results. Existing legacy listings retain their earlier policy.
 
This is a structured batch ingestion foundation, not a running crawler or scheduled job. No external sites were accessed. Collectors and reviewers require separate database logins; the application must bind submitted actor IDs to authenticated identities before exposing any endpoints. The administrator manages permissions outside this module. Review evidence is a human attestation, not independently verified truth. Exact duplicates are suppressed per permission; cross-source entity resolution and repeated observation of unchanged content require a subsequent refresh workflow. No private fields are intended; human screening of free text remains mandatory. Do not expose service credentials or collect data without permission. Real source adapters, rate limits, retry scheduling, audit and operational controls remain future work.

# DELMON LAVLAH MVP v0.1
DELMON — Хайх хэрэггүй. Асуухад л хангалттай.
Goal: Mongolian AI directory, free registration and listing, 1000 MNT per successful inquiry for people and organizations. Same-case clarification free. Emergency information free. Refund failed inquiries.
Architecture: isolated FastAPI backend, PostgreSQL, mobile-first Next.js PWA, authorized source adapters and future Data Core interface.
Days 1–3: domain model, inquiry state machine, tests, CI.
Days 4–7: organization profiles, categories, verified source records.
Days 8–14: Mongolian Cyrillic/Latin search, sourced answers, mobile UI.
Days 15–21: mock payment ledger, sandbox provider integration, refund and reconciliation.
Days 22–26: authorized collection, deduplication, freshness checks.
Days 27–30: pilot, cost measurement, security review. Targets not guarantees.
Acceptance: tenant isolation; no fabricated facts; explicit price and consent; no duplicate debit; failed inquiry refund; emergency free; no real payments or deployment without approval.

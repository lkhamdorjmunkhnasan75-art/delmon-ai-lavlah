Days 15–21: mock payment ledger, sandbox provider integration, refund and reconciliation.
Days 22–26: authorized collection, deduplication, freshness checks.
Days 27–30: pilot, cost measurement, security review. Targets not guarantees.
Acceptance: tenant isolation; no fabricated facts; explicit price and consent; no duplicate debit; failed inquiry refund; emergency free; no real payments or deployment without approval.

Organization subscription: 300000 MNT/year; free basic listing; paid service includes catalog management, lead/RFQ intake and analytics. Do not imply unlimited AI usage. Subscription entitlement and expiry must be tested; no live billing without approval.
